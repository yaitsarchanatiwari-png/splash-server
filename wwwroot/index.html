HOW TO SET UP SPLASH

Start here to get your key settings and local configs working perfectly in Minecraft.

ℹ️

Important: Click any key button, press the key or mouse button you want, then use Done / Save changes. Press Esc while capturing to cancel the binding or None.

🚀

1. QUICK START

  • Turn on "Enable configured keys" in the top header.
  • Your active Minecraft client is detected automatically.
  • Keep Splash minimized to the system tray while playing.
  • The green ACTIVE badge displays your running profile.
⌨️

2. BINDING & KEYS

  • Click any button, then press your desired key or mouse button.
  • Press Escape while capturing to clear or cancel a key.
  • Supports keyboard, side mouse buttons (M4/M5), and numpad.
  • Hotkeys operate with zero input delay in-game.
🖱️

3. PROFILE CLICKER

  • Click "+ Add" to create clicker cards into the next slot.
  • Configure Trigger key, CPS speed (1–500), and Left/Right.
  • Enable Hold Mode to hold a button continuously down.
  • Drag cards to reorder; click "..." to rename or delete.
🔄

4. SLOT SWITCH

  • Switch between 4 separate sequences using the 1, 2, 3, 4 tabs.
  • Set a single trigger to cycle hotbar weapon or tool slots.
  • Configure output keys with customizable millisecond delays.
  • Use "+ Add" to append outputs; drag cards to reorder.
👤

5. PROFILES & BACKUPS

  • Create dedicated profiles for PvP, Mace, Crystal, or Vanilla.
  • Assign Profile Hotkeys to switch setups instantly in-game.
  • Open Settings (gear icon) for Glow, Theme, and Sound FX.
  • Backup or restore your configuration with one click.
